1. THONG TIN TONG QUAN VE BO DU LIEU (DATASET PROVENANCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Ten de tai: Phan tich khong gian - kinh te thi truong phong tro TP.HCM & Binh Duong:
  Mo hinh dinh gia hop ly (Fair-Value Pricing) va He thong phat hien tin bat thuong
  (Scam & Good Deal Radar) tren nen tang Cho Tot.
- Sinh vien thuc hien: Truong Huy Hoang (MSSV: 24520574)
- Nen tang thu thap: Cho Tot Nha / Nha Tot (nhatot.com) - Chuyen muc Phong tro (cg=1050).
- Phuong phap thu thap: Cao phan tang tu dong qua API Gateway cua Cho Tot:
  GET https://gateway.chotot.com/v1/public/ad-listing
- Pham vi khong gian: 30 don vi hanh chinh (22 Quan/Huyen TP.HCM + 8 TP/Huyen Binh Duong).
- Pham vi thoi gian: Tin dang hoat dong tu 11/2024 den 11/2026 (95% o Quy 4/2026).
- Quy mo mau tho (Raw Data): 2.971 dong x 104 cot goc.
  + File CSV: data/raw/chotot_raw_hcm_bd.csv
  + File Excel: data/raw/chotot_raw_hcm_bd.xlsx
  + File JSON Lines: data/raw/chotot_raw_hcm_bd.jsonl
- Quy mo mau sach (Processed Data): 2.678 dong x 26 cot chuan hoa.
  + File CSV: data/processed/rental_scored_deals.csv
- Ty le giu lai sau lam sach: 90.17% (loai bo 9.83% spam, sai mien va ngoai lai).

2. HUONG DAN KIEM CHUNG DANH CHO GIANG VIEN (VERIFICATION GUIDE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Giang vien co the kiem chung tinh xac thuc cua bo du lieu de dang:
1. Mo file Excel 'data/raw/chotot_raw_hcm_bd.xlsx' hoac CSV 'chotot_raw_hcm_bd.csv'.
2. Cot thu 3 co ten la 'ad_url'. Nhan vao bat ky duong link nao trong cot nay:
   Format: https://www.nhatot.com/{list_id}.htm
3. Trinh duyet se mo truc tiep tin dang thuc te tren san Cho Tot voi day du hinh anh,
   so dien thoai lien he, dia chi va mo ta goc cua chu phong.

4. TU DIEN DU LIEU CHI TIET (26 COT CHUAN TRONG FILE NOP)
File luu tru: data/processed/rental_scored_deals.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[NHOM 1: DINH DANH & NGUON GOC]
1.  ad_id              (int64)      : Ma dinh danh duy nhat cua tin dang (Primary Key).
2.  list_id            (int64)      : Ma bai viet dung de ghep URL xem tin that tren web.
3.  account_id         (int64)      : Ma tai khoan nguoi dang (dung de phat hien spam).

[NHOM 2: THOI GIAN & KHONG GIAN DIA LY]
4.  list_time          (datetime)   : Thoi diem tin duoc cap nhat/day tin gan nhat (YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS).
5.  region_name        (string)     : Tinh/Thanh pho ('Tp Ho Chi Minh' hoac 'Binh Duong').
6.  area_name          (string)     : Quan/Huyen/Thanh pho truc thuoc (30 don vi hanh chinh).
7.  ward_name          (string)     : Phuong/Xa/Thi tran noi phong tro toa lac.
8.  street_name        (string)     : Ten duong/tuyen pho (dien 'Dang cap nhat' neu khuyet).
9.  longitude          (float64)    : Kinh do dia ly WGS84 (mien hop le [106.2, 107.2]).
10. latitude           (float64)    : Vi do dia ly WGS84 (mien hop le [10.3, 11.6]).
20. dist_to_q1_km      (float64)    : Khoang cach Haversine (km) toi Cho Ben Thanh, Quan 1 (10.7725, 106.6980).
21. dist_to_vnuhcm_km  (float64)    : Khoang cach Haversine (km) toi Khu Do thi DHQG-HCM (10.8756, 106.8007).

[NHOM 3: KINH TE, DIEN TICH & TINH TRANG PHONG]
11. size_m2            (float64)    : Dien tich su dung cua phong (m2), mien gia tri [10.0, 100.0].
12. price_million      (float64)    : BIEN MUC TIEU (Y) - Gia thue phong (Trieu VND/thang), mien [0.6, 13.5].
13. price_per_m2_k     (float64)    : Don gia thue tren moi m2 (Nghin VND/m2) = (price_million * 1000) / size_m2.
14. deposit_million    (float64)    : Tien dat coc giu phong (Trieu VND).
15. furnishing_status  (category)   : Tinh trang noi that ('Day du', 'Co ban', 'Nha trong', 'Khong ro').

[NHOM 4: TIEN ICH TRICH XUAT BANG NLP REGEX]
16. has_mezzanine      (int8: 0/1)  : 1 neu co gac lung, gac cao, gac duc (chiem 23.1% tong mau).
17. has_ac             (int8: 0/1)  : 1 neu co may lanh, dieu hoa (chiem 39.3% tong mau).
18. is_free_curfew     (int8: 0/1)  : 1 neu gio giac tu do, khoa van tay, khong chung chu (chiem 54.9%).
19. has_balcony_window (int8: 0/1)  : 1 neu co ban cong, cua so lon thoang mat (chiem 49.6%).

[NHOM 5: HANH VI, PHAN KHUC & MO HINH AI]
22. seller_type        (category)   : Nhom nguoi dang: 'Chinh chu' (840 tin), 'Moi gioi cong khai' (1.831 tin),
                                      'Moi gioi tra hinh' (7 tin).
23. market_cluster     (category)   : Phan khuc K-Means: 'Tier 1: Sinh vien/Binh dan' (985 tin),
                                      'Tier 2: Tien nghi tam trung' (604 tin), 'Tier 3: Studio/CHDV' (1.089 tin).
24. predicted_price    (float64)    : Gia thue hop ly (y_hat, Trieu VND) do mo hinh AI du doan.
25. valuation_gap_pct  (float64)    : Do lech gia (%) = ((price_million - predicted_price) / predicted_price) * 100.
26. deal_status        (category)   : Nhan quyet dinh phuc vu Case Study:
                                      - GOOD_DEAL   (37 tin) : Re hon >= 15% va do Chinh chu dang.
                                      - SCAM_RISK   (24 tin) : Re bat thuong >= 25% va nghi van bay tin ao.
                                      - OVERPRICED  (45 tin) : Dat hon >= 20% so voi gia hop ly.
                                      - FAIR_PRICE  (2.572 tin): Gia nam trong vung can bang thi truong.
